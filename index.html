About

I lead Use Research Data at Genomics England, the service enabling researchers to securely access and analyse data within the National Genomic Research Library.

My work sits across technology, product, operations, governance and organisational transformation, turning complex scientific and technical capabilities into services that work at scale.

Before Genomics England, I held leadership roles across digital health, AI and life sciences. Earlier in my career I spent seven years at the University of Oxford as a Senior Researcher and Lecturer, developing and evaluating digital health technologies across clinical studies and trials.
