# Carmelo Velardo biography

## Long version

Carmelo Velardo
Service Owner, Use Research Data – Genomics England

Carmelo Velardo is the Service Owner for Use Research Data at Genomics England, where he leads the service that enables researchers to access and analyse the data contained in the National Genomic Research Library. He works across product, technology, operations, governance, and stakeholder communities to ensure research services deliver scientific impact while meeting the needs of researchers, clinicians, and the wider genomics ecosystem.

Prior to Genomics England, Carmelo held senior leadership roles across digital health, artificial intelligence, and life sciences, including at Sensyne Health and LAB10x. He previously spent seven years at the University of Oxford as a Senior Researcher and Lecturer, developing digital health solutions for chronic conditions including diabetes, COPD, heart failure, and hypertension. His work contributed to more than 15 clinical studies and trials, including GDm-Health, the first digital health app and algorithm to receive NICE endorsement. Carmelo holds an MSc in Computer Engineering from Politecnico di Torino, a “Diplome d’Ingenieur” from EURECOM, and a PhD in Signal Processing from Telecom ParisTech. He has authored more than 70 peer reviewed scientific publications spanning biometrics, machine learning, digital health, and clinical research.
